Who we are

Building value.
Creating places.

Cypress and Myrtles Real Estate Ltd is an indigenous real estate company incorporated in 2016. We are committed to creating and connecting people with property opportunities across the Federal Capital Territory and its environs.

Our approach brings together thoughtful development, practical real estate insight and a focus on long-term value. Whether you are looking for a place to call home or considering your next property investment, we aim to make the journey clearer and more rewarding.
